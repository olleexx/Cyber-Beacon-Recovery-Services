About Us

A small team focused on one problem: helping scam victims understand what happened and what to do next.

Our approach

Every case begins with an assessment of the reported incident and the available documentation. Based on what we find, we outline investigative steps and possible reporting or recovery channels.

We do not make promises we cannot keep. Our job is to give you clear information so you can decide what to do.

  • Transparency. Honest communication about findings, limitations, costs and options.
  • Confidentiality. Information and documents are handled responsibly.
  • No pressure. We help you decide what is right for you.
  • Evidence first. Conclusions follow the records, not guesses.

Our values

Privacy

Respect
