Privacy Policy

How Cyber Beacon Recovery Services handles the information you share with us. Last updated: October 10, 2026.

Who we are

Cyber Beacon Recovery Services provides investigative guidance and case documentation for victims of online fraud and crypto scams. You can contact us at cyberbeaconrecoveryservices@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Details you give in our case form: your name, email address, phone number, country, a description of the incident, dates, the website or platform involved, the type and estimated amount of funds lost, the evidence you hold, and whether you have used another recovery service.
  • Messages you send us by email or live chat.
  • Files you choose to upload with the case form, such as screenshots, receipts and statements. These reach us as email attachments.
  • Basic technical data, such as browser type and IP address, collected by our website and the services listed below.

How we use it

  • To assess your case and decide what help we can realistically offer.
  • To contact you about your case and answer your questions.
  • To keep records of our work and protect our service against fraud and abuse.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We use trusted service providers to run our website: a form delivery service that passes your submission to our email, our email provider, and a live chat provider if you use chat. They process data only to provide those services. We may share case documentation with exchanges, banks, legal professionals or law enforcement when you ask us to, or where the law requires it.

What not to send us

Never send passwords, seed phrases, private keys or full card numbers, and please do not upload identity documents unless we ask. We will never ask for passwords, seed phrases or private keys.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to assess your case and keep appropriate records, then delete or anonymize it, unless we are required to keep it longer.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may be able to ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to object to how we use it. Contact us using the email above and we will respond.

Cookies

Our live chat provider may set cookies to keep your chat session. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
